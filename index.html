About VihangX

Small drones. Big dreams.

VihangX Drone Club is a student-led technical community at Sandipani Technical Campus, Latur. We create a practical environment where students learn, build, innovate and explore drone technology through shared experience.

✓ Practical technical skills✓ Hands-on projects✓ Workshops & competitions✓ Teamwork & research
Club charter

Purpose with a clear direction.

VihangX is built around practical learning, responsible technology and work that prepares students for real-world challenges.

01 / AIM

Make practice possible.

Provide students with practical knowledge and hands-on experience in drone technology, electronics, programming, design, safety and innovation.

02 / VISION

Build a capable community.

Create a skilled, innovative and responsible student community capable of developing safe, useful, technology-driven drone solutions for real-world challenges.

03 / MISSION

Learn. Build. Fly.

Create a practical learning environment that develops technical skills, teamwork, creativity and responsible practices in drone technology.

04 / OBJECTIVES

Grow beyond the classroom.

Develop technical capability, leadership, safe practices, research confidence and a portfolio of meaningful drone-based work.
