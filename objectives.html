Our objectives

Nine commitments to every member.

These objectives form the working standard for how the club learns, builds, leads and innovates.

01Develop students’ knowledge of drone technology.
02Provide hands-on experience in drone building and operation.
03Develop basic skills in electronics, programming and drone systems.
04Encourage innovation, creativity and problem-solving.
05Promote teamwork, leadership and communication skills.
06Educate members about safe and responsible drone practices.
07Develop innovative drone-based projects for real-world applications.
08Participate in technical competitions, exhibitions and events.
09Encourage research and experimentation in emerging drone technologies.
