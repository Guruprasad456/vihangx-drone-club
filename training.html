Training

From drone basics to confident flight.

A practical learning path that connects component knowledge, system-building and responsible operation.

Learning path

Three stages. One complete system.

Begin with the building blocks, connect them into a working aircraft and then learn how controlled flight happens.

01 / DRONE BASICS

What is a drone?

Understand an Unmanned Aerial Vehicle and the basics of propulsion, control, stability, power, sensors and flight controllers.

02 / COMPONENTS

Know every part.

Learn the role of the frame, motors, propellers, flight controller, ESC and battery in a typical quadcopter.

03 / FLIGHT

Control with purpose.

Practice how throttle, roll, pitch and yaw work together to create lift, balance, control and direction.

Training outcomes

Skills with real-world value.

Members gain a foundation for projects, research, internships, higher studies and a wide range of technology careers.

01 / TECHNICAL

Build technical skill

Develop knowledge in electronics, programming, design, control systems and testing.

02 / COLLABORATIVE

Lead with others

Grow responsibility, communication and confidence while contributing to team work.

03 / PRACTICAL

Create a portfolio

Demonstrate, present and compete through practical project work.
